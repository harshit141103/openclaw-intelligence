# Memory: Craig Figueroa

## Personal Profile

Craig Figueroa is the only large-animal vet across Wester Ross, Lochalsh, and parts of Sutherland, working out of a converted stone crofthouse in Strathcarron that he owns with his partner Fiona Drummond. He is a fifth-generation Strathcarron Figueroa, born in Inverness, British citizen, with a Spanish thread that comes from his grandmother and a basic Norwegian thread that comes from a veterinary exchange in Tromsø. He holds a BVMS from the University of Glasgow (2011), is registered with the RCVS (MRCVS 7104382), and has held Official Veterinarian (OV) status with the OCQ(V) (TB) qualification since 2016 for APHA TB testing work. He drives a kitted-out Land Rover Defender as a mobile clinic and is slowly restoring a Series III in the outbuilding adjacent to the dispensary.

He is a patient, deadpan, action-over-words operator. His default off-duty mode is a hill walk, a cold swim in Loch Carron, a glass of anejo tequila after dinner, and a Rodrigo y Gabriela record in the kitchen. He carries the difficult winter of 2024/25 closer to the surface than he lets on, when he lost two calves in a single week.

## Work & Projects

Figueroa Veterinary Services is a sole-trader large-animal practice covering cattle (beef suckler herds), sheep (hill flocks), Highland ponies, and the occasional goat herd across roughly forty-five farms and crofts in a sixty-mile radius. The home office sits in the crofthouse, with the Land Rover Defender as the mobile clinic and a small dispensary outbuilding for stock and storage.

- **Career timeline**: BVMS, University of Glasgow, July 2011; August 2011 to June 2014, assistant vet in mixed practice at Dingwall Veterinary Group; July 2014 to May 2016, large-animal associate under Dr. Jean MacKenzie in Ullapool covering Wester Ross; June 2016, took over the area from Dr. Jean MacKenzie as sole proprietor of Figueroa Veterinary Services.
- **Services**: routine herd health, TB testing, fertility work, lambing and calving support, surgical procedures, emergency call-outs.
- **Schedule**: farm visits Monday through Friday, emergencies 24/7/365, admin evenings and Saturday mornings, no fixed office hours.
- **Autumn Herd Health Round 2026**: comprehensive assessments, vaccinations, condition scoring. Three farms with complex health histories under close monitoring: Duncan Fraser at Achnasheen, Iain Campbell at Applecross, and a newer client near Torridon.
- **Autumn TB Testing Round**: 28 herds across Lochcarron cluster, Torridon and Applecross, and remote Sutherland, running October 1 to the November 20 APHA completion deadline, compliance and paperwork intensive, coordinated with Rhona Matheson.
- **Practice Modernisation**: migrating to a cloud-based vet-specific platform for clinical notes and drug dispensing logs, offline-first because of remote signal. Pilot of 10 farms done, full migration target December 19, 2026.
- **Professional development**: Highland Veterinary Congress in Harrogate October 14 to 16, considering advanced training in bovine reproductive ultrasonography.
