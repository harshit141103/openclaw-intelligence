# Heartbeat: Craig Figueroa

## Recurring Events

### Daily

- 5:30 AM porridge, tea, message check, road and weather scan, route plan for the day.
- Morning farm visit block from roughly 7:30 AM, paced by drive time and geography.
- Late-afternoon return to the crofthouse for clinical notes, drug dispensing log entries, and any APHA admin.
- 10:30 PM target bedtime, phone charging by the bed, on-call alert tone enabled.

### Weekly

- **Monday**: routine farm calls and herd health checks. Light admin in the afternoon. Daily Alejandro check-in on the drive home through Lochcarron.
- **Tuesday**: TB testing or fertility work in scheduled geographic blocks. Long driving day, flask and lunch packed. Check on Alejandro on the way home.
- **Wednesday**: morning farm visits, protected admin block from 2 PM for invoicing, APHA correspondence, supply ordering. Quiet evening with Fiona.
- **Thursday**: farm visits, protected afternoon for complex cases and phone consultations. Evening call with Isabel.
- **Friday**: lighter farm round in the morning, week wrap-up in the afternoon, pint with local friends at the Lochcarron Hotel in the evening at the corner table near the fire.
- **Saturday**: practice admin and drug stock check in the morning, hill walk with Fiona or visit to Isabel and the children in Plockton in the afternoon.
- **Sunday**: walk with Fiona, lunch at Alejandro and Elena's croft in Lochcarron, weekly review and meal prep in the afternoon, on-call but hoping for quiet.

### Monthly

- 1st of the month: financial review ,  practice income, expenses, tax set-aside.
- Mid-month drug stock inventory and supplier order placement so deliveries land before the next call-out block.
- One Saturday a month: Inverness supply run, including any gear or restoration parts pickup.
- Monthly extended call with Jean MacKenzie for complex case discussion when one is open.

### Quarterly

- Quarterly check-in call with Douglas Mackay at Mackay & Co. on tax set-aside and projected income.
- Quarterly practice review against the digitisation milestones and the 35K savings target.
- Quarterly Land Rover Defender service and mobile clinic kit refresh.

### Seasonal / Variable

- Spring: lambing season, dawn-to-dark hours, locum cover with Graeme Sutherland during the worst weeks.
- Summer: longer daylight, Munro-bagging windows, Loch Carron swims when the water allows.
- Autumn: TB testing round across 28 herds from October 1 to the November 20 APHA completion deadline, agricultural show season, cattle housing visits.
- Winter: short days, icy single-track roads, satellite phone packed, hill walks restricted to weather windows.
- On-call: 24/7/365 baseline, with locum cover only during holidays and the Harrogate congress week.

### Annual

- January: annual checkup with Dr. Alison Grant at Lochcarron Medical Practice, RCVS registration renewal, annual plan review with Fiona.
- February: clinical records archive and APHA annual reporting.
- October: Highland Veterinary Congress, Harrogate.
- November: autumn TB round completion and reporting to APHA by the November 20 deadline, Fiona's birthday on the 14th at The Plockton Hotel.
- December: Alejandro's birthday on the 5th, family Christmas at the Lochcarron croft on the 25th, on-call but hoping for quiet.
- Physiotherapy with Kirsty Murray in Inverness every six weeks across the year.
