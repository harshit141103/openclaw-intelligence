# Memory: Ben Cox

## Personal Profile

- **Birthplace:** St. Albans, Vermont, born 1965.
- **Occupation:** Self-employed master carpenter running Cox Custom Woodwork: custom cabinetry, timber framing, restoration, and finish carpentry. A one-man operation with occasional subcontractors for larger jobs.
- **Education:** High school diploma, St. Albans (1983). Apprenticed under master carpenter Paul Dufresne for four years (1984 to 1988).
- **Languages:** English (native).
- **Marital status:** Married to Diane Cox (nee Trudeau) since 1991, 35 years together. Two grown children.
- **Character:** Vermont stubborn and Vermont honest. He says what he means and does not say it twice, distrusts new for its own sake, values what earns its place, and treats the shop on the property as the load-bearing center of his daily life.

## Key Relationships

1. **Diane Cox (Wife, 58)**: Works part-time as a school librarian at Craftsbury Academy and manages Ben's business books in QuickBooks on the household laptop. They are a team: she handles the business side, he handles the wood. She is the more social and organized of the two, and the reason Ben has any friends at all (his words).
2. **Keith Cox (Son, 32)**: Lives in Burlington, software engineer at a tech startup. Close in a quiet way: visits monthly, maintains the Cox Custom Woodwork website, and understands his dad even when they do not share interests.
3. **Marie Cox-Gagne (Daughter, 29)**: Lives in Montpelier, elementary school teacher, married to Tom Gagne, no kids yet. Warmer and more communicative; she and Ben have a natural ease and she calls weekly.
4. **Tom Gagne (Son-in-law)**: Married to Marie. Reachable through her.
5. **Dr. James Whitfield (Primary care, 56)**: Copley Hospital, Morrisville. Manages Ben's sleep apnea and general health. Practical and no-nonsense, a good match.
6. **Dr. LeBlanc (Dentist)**: Hardwick Dental, cleanings every 6 months.
7. **Pete Lafleur (Best friend, 62)**: Retired dairy farmer down the road, friends since the 1990s. Saturday morning coffee in Ben's shop. They talk about weather, wood, and whether the Red Sox can hit this year, never feelings.
8. **The Hendersons (Current client, Stowe)**: Custom kitchen cabinet project, multi-day delivery and install.
9. **Craftsbury professor (Current client)**: Built-in bookshelves.
10. **Paul Dufresne (Mentor, retired)**: The master carpenter who taught Ben from 1984 to 1988. Still in St. Albans, still receives a Christmas card every year.

## Work & Projects

- **Business:** Cox Custom Woodwork. Sole proprietor, 30 years. No advertising since 2004; reputation carries.
- **Services:** Custom cabinetry, built-ins, timber framing, restoration carpentry, finish work.
- **Shop:** Detached heated workshop on the property, equipped with table saw, jointer, planer, drill press, band saw, and an extensive hand tool collection.
- **Current projects:**
  - Custom kitchen cabinets for the Hendersons in Stowe (major project, December install).
  - Built-in bookshelves for a Craftsbury professor.
  - Restoration of an 1840s barn in Greensboro (planned for summer 2027).
- **Revenue:** Gross approximately $85,000 a year. Net after materials and subcontractor costs approximately $55,000 (shop insurance, the truck, and overhead come out of the household budget, not deducted here).

- **Combined household:** approximately $73,000 net.
- **Workflow:** Ben handles design, estimating, and execution. Diane handles invoicing, taxes, and the QuickBooks books. Keith maintains the simple website.

## Health & Wellness

- **Obstructive Sleep Apnea (moderate):** Diagnosed 2021 after Diane insisted on a sleep study, AHI 22 events/hour. Uses a CPAP (ResMed AirSense 11) nightly with good compliance (about 7 hours), with a nasal pillow mask he finds most comfortable. Still hates the mask but the energy difference is undeniable. CPAP data reviewed at annual appointments.
- **Hypertension (mild, improving):** Diagnosed 2021, BP 148/92 at diagnosis, now 136/84 on lisinopril 10mg in the morning. Dr. Whitfield is considering discontinuing if the weight loss continues.
- **Weight:** BMI 29, down from 31 at diagnosis. Target is 15 more pounds. Slow progress.
- **Primary care:** Dr. James Whitfield, Copley Hospital, Morrisville. Annual physical plus sleep apnea review each February.
- **Dental:** Dr. LeBlanc, Hardwick Dental, every 6 months.
- **Medications and supplements:** Lisinopril 10mg (morning), Vitamin D3 2000 IU (daily for Vermont winters), multivitamin (daily, at Diane's insistence).
- **Exercise:** The job itself, plus after-dinner walks with Diane (30 minutes, 4 to 5 nights a week), firewood splitting, and occasional winter snowshoeing. No gym.
- **Sleep:** Much improved since CPAP, 7 to 7.5 hours, bed by 9:30 PM and up by 5:00 AM.
- **Dietary (medical):** No strict restrictions; the 15-pound weight-loss goal drives the choices. Diane cooks healthier now (more fish, less red meat, more vegetables) and Ben complies with mild grumbling.

## Interests & Hobbies

- **Woodworking:** The obvious passion that is also the job; he has never settled whether the shop on a Sunday is leisure or just more of the week, and holds Sunday pieces to the same standard as paid work.
- **Fishing:** The cleaner pleasure. Trout in the small streams he has worked since boyhood, standing in cold water with no deadline and no client. Releases most of what he catches.
- **Splitting firewood:** The satisfaction of a clean halve and a stack growing into something he can count.
- **Reading:** Woodworking magazines (Fine Woodworking, Popular Woodworking) he half-argues with, plus occasional WWII and Vermont history. Physical copies, read in his shop chair.
- **Music:** Classic rock (CCR, Stones, Zeppelin), bluegrass, old country on the shop radio, VPR for news.
- **TV:** Minimal. Red Sox games (mostly radio), the occasional documentary with Diane, news.

## Preferences

- **Shopping:** Local hardware stores. Hardwick Lumber for general material, Burlington for specialty wood. Diane handles groceries.
- **Aesthetic:** Form is what is left standing after you strip away everything that does not earn its place. Clean joinery over ornament, oiled wood over paint.
- **Clothes:** Flannel, canvas, boots resoled more than once, a barn coat older than one of the dogs. The same uniform for the shop and for town.
- **Food and drink:** Three cups of black Green Mountain coffee brewed at 5:00 AM. Cheese (Vermont cheddar, obviously), crackers, apples, and jerky. Two to three local craft beers a week (Hill Farmstead when available), with the occasional whiskey with Pete.
- **Eating out:** Craftsbury General Store for coffee and a sandwich, Parker Pie in West Glover on Friday nights, Positive Pie in Hardwick for date night.
- **Cooking:** Diane handles cooking; Ben grills and makes breakfast, and his French toast is genuinely good.
- **Weather sensitivity:** High. Outdoor work and the drive to job sites both hinge on the forecast.
